# Gemini Compass

## Discover Yourself. Understand Yourself. Build Your Life.

Gemini Compass is a personal growth app that helps people explore their personality, understand their strengths and challenges, develop useful habits, and work toward a successful life.

### Our Starting Point
We are starting with Gemini, using astrology as a theme for self-reflection while focusing on each person's real experiences, choices, and goals.

### What the App Will Help Users Do
- Explore their strengths and areas for growth.
- Reflect on their habits, emotions, and decision-making.
- Discover practical ways to improve their daily lives.
- Set personal goals and track their progress.
- Build confidence through guided self-discovery.

### Our Vision
To help people understand themselves better and turn that understanding into practical action.

### Project Status
Early development — building the foundation one step at a time.
